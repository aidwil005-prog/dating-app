# MatchSphere — Brand Guidelines

## Product
MatchSphere is an 18+ dating app for **iOS and Android only**. The website is a marketing destination, **not** an app for registering, matching or chatting.

**Tagline:** Meet People Who Match Your World.

## Brand colors
Only use the ten requested pastel colors and deep warm-brown text:

| Token | Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | Seashell | `#f8edeb` | Main website / app background |
| `--bg-alt` | Soft Blush | `#fae1dd` | Alternate sections |
| `--bg-warm` | Soft Linen | `#ece4db` | Warm neutral sections |
| `--card` | Powder Petal | `#ffe5d9` | Primary cards |
| `--card-2` | Almond Silk | `#fcd5ce` | Secondary cards |
| `--border` | Alabaster Grey | `#e8e8e4` | Dividers |
| `--sage` | Alabaster Sage | `#d8e2dc` | Cool contrast |
| `--accent` | Powder Blush | `#fec5bb` | Primary buttons |
| `--accent-2` | Peach Glow | `#fec89a` | Highlights |
| `--chip` | Peach Fuzz | `#ffd7ba` | Small badges |
| `--text` | Warm Brown | `#382d2a` | Text on all pastels |

Body text must meet WCAG AA contrast with its actual surface. No white text on light peach/pink backgrounds. Keep shadows restrained and motion optional.

## Typography
- Display: licensed **Sailoors** only if supplied, else Playfair Display / Georgia / serif.
- Body UI: licensed **Proxima Nova** only if supplied, else Montserrat / system sans-serif.
- Marketing type scale: H1 72/40, H2 48/32, H3 24/20, body 18/16 desktop/mobile.
- App uses platform-appropriate system fonts by default to preserve readability and speed.

## Layout and motion
- Marketing: announcement, sticky nav, hero with **illustrative** mobile app previews, numbered how-it-works, bento features, benefits, illustrations (never fake testimonials), journal, app-store availability and footer.
- Mobile: safe-area-aware compact navigation, clear card hierarchy, 44px+ taps, keyboard support and simple motion. Not a compressed web layout.
- Radii: pills for buttons, ~24px cards, ~20px images; max 1200px marketing content width.
- Keep the product free of dark patterns, fake urgency, fake testimonials or fake compatibility claims.

## Content and safety
Clear coming-soon status until real store links are verified. No fake signups or backend claims. App target audience is 18+, but server-verified eligibility, privacy protection, genuine consent before messaging and moderation must exist before a public dating release. Pricing cannot gate safety or essential messaging.
