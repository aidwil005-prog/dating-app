# Product decision — 31 October 2026

**MatchSphere is mobile exclusive (iOS and Android).** A public **marketing website** remains, but it is **not a dating web application**.

## Allowed on marketing website

Overview, features, illustrated mobile screens, how it works, safety principles, journal, FAQ, and verified App Store/Google Play download links when the applications are published. Until then, the website shows clear "Coming soon" indicators and does not collect signup or waitlist data.

## Required inside mobile apps

Adult identity assurance and sign-in, user profiles, discovery, interest communities, approved requests, genuine consent-bound chats, reporting/blocking, privacy controls and user notifications. Today these are **fictional demonstrations** only.

## Do not

- Reintroduce `/app/` with browser-based dating functionality.
- Link to unverified App Store or Play Store pages.
- Claim there is a working waitlist or newsletter backend.
- Describe demo profile pictures, conversations, compatibility results or fake communities as real users.
- Equate a self-entered date of birth or consent checkbox with age verification.
- Pretend a static preview is ready for a public dating launch.
