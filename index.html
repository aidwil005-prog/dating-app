05WHAT MATTERS

A softer way
to meet.
A clearer way
to choose.

♥

Feeling seen should never mean giving up your boundaries. We're designing around respect from the beginning.

Mutual by design

Private messages are meant to open only after two people agree to connect.

Safety isn't a premium extra

Blocking, reporting, and essential messaging should be available without a paywall.

Honesty over hype

Clear relationship intentions, thoughtful privacy, and understandable matching reasons.

These are product commitments, not live protections. Independent 18+ age verification, backend enforcement, and moderation must be implemented before real users join.
