# Human-Centered Dating Psychology — MatchSphere

## Principles
- Optimize for meaningful, consensual conversations rather than infinite sessions, public popularity counters, or streak pressure.
- Ask users to disclose relationship goals and status, including informed consensual non-monogamy; do not infer intent from behavior.
- Provide transparent reasons for interest-based matches; explain that percentages are heuristic, not scientific predictions of success.
- Do not diagnose or label users with attachment disorders or styles based on app behavior.
- No artificial scarcity, manipulative notifications, fake likes, false online statuses, or algorithmic pretenses.
- Support easy connection decline, block, report, notification controls, and taking breaks.
- Use communities and shared hobbies for conversation starters, not as substitutes for preference, safety, or consent.
- Visual design: readable typography, calm hierarchy, restrained gradients, and optional motion. Support prefers-reduced-motion.

## Research interpretation
- https://www.attachmentproject.com/research/psychology-dating-apps-study/ : Large self-report survey on user motivations; associations and selection effects do not establish causality. Avoid universal assumptions about users.
- https://medium.com/@inappstory/the-psychology-of-mobile-app-design-how-colors-fonts-and-layouts-impact-app-perfomance-9a2e0f39e1d9 : Practical color, typography, grouping and layout concepts; color associations should not be treated as guaranteed behavior effects.
- https://github.com/dickwu/apple-design-skill : Inspiration for codifying clear, accessibility-minded design rules, not an Apple-approved theme.

## Product review questions
1. Is connection mutual, clearly understood, and reversible?
2. Does this feature respect adults' autonomy instead of encouraging dependency?
3. Are match reasons accurate and auditable?
4. Is any profile, member count, or recommendation fictional? Label it as demo.
5. Does every essential flow work without motion and with keyboard access?
6. Are safety operations actually server-enforced? If not, mark as demo only.
